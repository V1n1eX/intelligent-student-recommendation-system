THE METHOD

From student profile
to useful possibilities.

A content-based recommendation workflow that compares student context with programme and resource catalogue data, then makes the reasons visible.

THE WORKFLOW

Every recommendation
starts with context.

01

PROFILE INPUTS

Represent the student

The student profile brings together academic background, subjects and grades, interests, preferred fields, career goals, learning style, and study mode. Text fields are combined into a profile summary for contextual comparison.

Academic backgroundInterestsCareer goalsStudy preferences
◉Student profileStructured fields + profile text
02

CATALOGUE DATA

Describe each option

Programme entries include fields such as academic area, requirements, subjects, career opportunities, and interest tags. Resource entries include subject, format, skills, and interest tags. Staff can manage catalogue records through Django Admin.

ProgrammesLearning resourcesEditable catalogue
▤Catalogue entriesActive programmes + resources
03

SIMILARITY & SCORING

Compare on multiple signals

For programmes, the engine combines Jaccard overlap across interests, subjects or academic field, and careers with qualification and study compatibility. TF-IDF with cosine similarity adds a contextual text signal. Resource matching uses interests, subjects, skills, learning preference, and text similarity.

Jaccard similarityTF-IDFCosine similarity
⌕Content-based matchingStructured + text features
04

RANKING & EXPLANATIONS

Return options students can inspect

Results are sorted by score and include matching factors, an explanation, skill gaps, and suggested next steps. Students can review programme recommendations and learning resources in the dashboard and recommendations pages.

Ranked resultsMatch factorsSuggested next steps
✓Explainable resultsReviewable guidance

PROGRAMME SCORING

Signals work together
to build a match.

Structured programme weights cover interests, academic alignment, career alignment, qualification compatibility, and study preference. A text similarity signal is also added, and the final score is capped at 100.

Resource recommendations use a separate weighting across interests (30%), subject relevance (25%), skills (20%), learning preference (15%), and text similarity (10%).

DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Useful guidance
keeps people in control.

The system is intended to support exploration and discussion. Recommendations do not replace formal admissions decisions or academic advising.

01

Explain the fit

Show the factors behind a suggestion so students can decide what feels relevant.

02

Keep data editable

Update programme and resource catalogue entries through the administration interface.

03

Use as a guide

Scores are prototype signals for exploration, not an admissions outcome or guarantee.
